Symptrace — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Symptrace is a health diary that runs entirely on your device. This policy describes what it does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing, and nothing you record leaves your device.

What Symptrace stores

Symptrace stores, in a database on your device only:

  • the treatments and symptoms you configure, including their names, icons, colours and default doses;
  • the entries you report — what it was, the date and time, a dose, a 1–5 severity, and any note you type;
  • illnesses you have closed, together with the entries archived under them;
  • your preferences, such as whether the Face ID lock is on.

What Symptrace does not do

  • It has no account and no sign-up. You are never asked for a name, an email address or a phone number.
  • It contains no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.
  • It does not read or write Apple Health, Contacts, Photos, Location or your calendar.
  • It does not upload your log, back it up to any server, or share it with the developer or anyone else.
  • The developer has no access to your data and no way to obtain it.

The one network request

When — and only when — you open More illnesses in Settings, Symptrace downloads a public list of illness templates from raw.githubusercontent.com. That request sends nothing but itself: no health data, no identifiers, no analytics. GitHub, as the host, may log the request as any web server does; see GitHub's own privacy statement. If you never open that screen, Symptrace makes no network requests at all.

Face ID and Touch ID

If you turn the lock on, Symptrace asks iOS to verify you. Biometric data is handled entirely by iOS and never reaches the app; Symptrace only learns whether the check succeeded.

What you export

The PDF export produces a file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. What happens to it then is your choice — where you send it, and whether your private notes are included, are both decided by you at the moment of export.

Your control over your data

You can delete any entry individually, delete every entry at once, delete a closed illness with everything archived under it, or reset the app entirely, all from Settings. Deleting the app from your device removes its database with it. Because the data never leaves your device, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and no data request to make.

Children

Symptrace does not knowingly collect anything from anyone, of any age, because it does not collect anything at all.

Not medical advice

Symptrace is a diary. It does not diagnose anything, does not recommend treatment, and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
