Symptrace is a private log for an illness: what you took, what you felt, and when.

TWO TAPS TO REPORT
Pick treatment or symptom, tap what it was, save. The date is already today and the time is already now, with one-tap shortcuts for 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours ago when you are catching up. Treatments can carry a dose, symptoms a 1-5 severity, and anything can carry a note.

SET UP IN SECONDS
Pick an illness and Symptrace creates the treatments and symptoms usually tracked for it. Rename, recolour, reorder or hide any of them afterwards - they become ordinary items on your list. Or add your own, one at a time.

SEE HOW IT IS GOING
History groups everything by day over 7, 30 or 90 days, or all of it, with a chart of how much you logged each day and how bad the symptoms were on average.

CLOSE AN ILLNESS WHEN IT IS OVER
Closing files the whole log away - you can still read it and export it - and starts you clean for whatever comes next. Closed illnesses stay in Settings for as long as you want them.

SOMETHING TO HAND YOUR DOCTOR
Export any period as a PDF: one section per day, one line per entry, ready to print or send. You choose whether your private notes go with it.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything stays on your device. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics and no ads, and your log is never uploaded anywhere. Turn on Face ID and the app locks the moment it leaves the screen.

Symptrace is free and open source: github.com/nowheremanmail/trackmyillness

Symptrace is a diary, not a doctor. It does not diagnose anything, it does not tell you what to take, and it is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
